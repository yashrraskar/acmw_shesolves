About the event

A puzzle worth showing up for.

SheSolves is ACM-W's annual gathering for anyone who wants to get better at thinking in code. It's part workshop, part build sprint, part community — one day where you trade the syllabus for real problems, work alongside mentors from industry and academia, and leave with something you actually built.

The event is open to students of all experience levels. No prior competitive-programming background required — just curiosity and a willingness to get stuck on something and solve it anyway.

  • Beginner-friendly tracks alongside advanced challenges
  • Mentors from partner companies and ACM-W alumnae
  • Everything you build, you keep — portfolio-ready by evening

The day, traced out

Event schedule

One path, a few branches. Pick a track when the schedule forks — you can switch at the break.

  1. 9:00 – 9:30

    Check-in & welcome coffee

    Badge pickup, swag, and settling in.

  2. 9:30 – 10:00

    Opening ceremony & keynote

    "Why We Solve" — a keynote from an ACM-W alumna now building in industry.

  3. 10:00 – 11:30

    Tracks open

    Track A · DSA Sprint Track B · Build-a-thon kickoff
  4. 11:30 – 11:45

    Break

    Stretch, refill, swap tracks if you'd like.

  5. 11:45 – 1:00

    Workshop: ML for everyone

    A hands-on, no-prerequisites intro to machine learning fundamentals.

  6. 1:00 – 2:00

    Lunch & networking

    Meet mentors, sponsors, and fellow participants over food.

  7. 2:00 – 4:00

    Build sprint continues

    Heads-down time with mentors circulating for help.

  8. 4:00 – 4:30

    Panel: Women shaping tech

    An open Q&A with engineers and researchers on the panel.

  9. 4:30 – 5:30

    Demos & judging

    Show what you built. Judges circulate table to table.

  10. 5:30 – 6:00

    Awards & closing

    Prizes, certificates, and a group photo.
